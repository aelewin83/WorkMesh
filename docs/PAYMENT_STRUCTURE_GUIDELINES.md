# Payment Structure Guidelines

Engagement structures should feel lightweight and negotiable.

## Typical fit

- Media and Production: Day Rate, Weekly/Retainer
- Research, Analysis, and Advisory: Flat Fee, Negotiable/Open Proposal
- Executive Assistance and Coordination: Hourly, Weekly/Retainer
- Logistics and Transport: Hourly, Day Rate
- Events and Staffing: Hourly, Day Rate

## UX rules

- Rates may be blank, ranges, or flexible language.
- Rate visibility is user-controlled.
- Protected settlement is agreement-based, not instant labor checkout.
- Open proposal flows finalize compensation during agreement creation.
