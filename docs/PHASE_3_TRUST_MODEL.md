# Phase 3 Trust Model

Phase 3 expands security beyond technical compromise into trust collapse, reputation manipulation, coercion, reports, and private-network abuse.

Risks considered:

- Fake accounts and invite abuse.
- Reputation farming and repeated low-value collusion.
- Social engineering, impersonation, and extortion.
- Fake escrow or payment disputes.
- Malicious coordination attempts.
- Account recovery abuse.
- Doxxing through disclosure, exact location, or relationship clues.
- Relationship graph exposure through public collaboration history.

Relai should resist these risks without creating a public social graph or surveillance-heavy moderation system.
