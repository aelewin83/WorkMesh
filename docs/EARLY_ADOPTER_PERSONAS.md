# Early Adopter Personas

## Independent contributor

Works in writing, research, media, local sourcing, executive coordination, or logistics. They value privacy, fast settlement clarity, and selective disclosure over public profile building.

## Trusted requester

Needs discreet operational support, clear agreements, secure messaging, and payment visibility. They are not looking for an endless public labor directory.

## Private beta steward

Moderates invites, watches disputes, and helps liquidity form without turning the platform into a surveillance-heavy marketplace.
